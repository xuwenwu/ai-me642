# NVE Energy Conservation Rubric

Total: 100 points.

This rubric aligns with the app's default grading categories.

## Conceptual Correctness: 20 Points

- 18-20: Correctly explains NVE, total-energy conservation, timestep stability, and relevant physical caveats.
- 14-17: Mostly correct, with minor gaps in terminology or physical reasoning.
- 8-13: Recognizes energy drift but gives incomplete or loosely connected explanation.
- 0-7: Misunderstands the ensemble, conservation expectation, or scientific claim.

## Script and Workflow Correctness: 20 Points

- 18-20: Input, run workflow, and optional scripts are coherent, reproducible, and consistent with the stated task.
- 14-17: Workflow is mostly reproducible with small missing details.
- 8-13: Important setup or run details are unclear.
- 0-7: Files are missing, contradictory, or not usable for review.

## Simulation Validation: 25 Points

- 23-25: Required evidence is complete; validation checks and thermo plots are interpreted accurately.
- 18-22: Evidence is mostly complete; warnings are addressed with reasonable caveats.
- 10-17: Validation was run but important warnings, missing columns, or drift evidence are weakly discussed.
- 0-9: Validation is absent, ignored, or contradicted by the student's claim.

## Interpretation and Communication: 20 Points

- 18-20: Interpretation is specific, cautious, evidence-based, and clear about uncertainty.
- 14-17: Interpretation is understandable but lacks some detail or caveat handling.
- 8-13: Interpretation repeats generic claims without tying them to uploaded evidence.
- 0-7: Interpretation is empty, unsupported, or misleading.

## Reproducibility and AI Disclosure: 15 Points

- 14-15: Package is reproducible; AI assistance is disclosed with accepted/rejected/manual-edit/validation details.
- 10-13: Reproducibility or disclosure is mostly adequate with minor missing details.
- 5-9: Some files or AI-use details are present but incomplete.
- 0-4: Major reproducibility evidence is missing or AI use is undisclosed when clearly relevant.

## Feedback Sentence Starters

- "Your strongest evidence is..."
- "The validation warning matters because..."
- "Before making this claim stronger, you would need..."
- "Your AI disclosure is strong because..."
- "Revise the interpretation to connect the plot trend to..."
